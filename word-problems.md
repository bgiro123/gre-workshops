<div class="sec "><p class="kicker">Choosing variables</p><h2>Pick variables that do the remembering for you</h2><div class="cards3"><div class="card" v-click><p class="num">1</p><h3>Name them clearly</h3><p class="txt2">Use <i>M</i> for Maya and <i>d</i> for distance, not <i>x</i> and <i>y</i>. You’ll misread fewer of your own equations.</p></div><div class="card" v-click><p class="num">2</p><h3>Solve for the target</h3><p class="txt2">When you can, make the variable the quantity the question asks for. That saves a final step and a trap.</p></div><div class="card" v-click><p class="num">3</p><h3>Build from one anchor</h3><p class="txt2">If everything is described relative to one quantity, use one variable and write the rest in terms of it.</p></div></div></div>
