<div class="sec dark center"><div class="bar"></div><p class="kicker">Part 3</p><h1>Smart moves</h1><p class="lead">No penalty for guessing, and you can go back.</p></div>

---
layout: none
---

<div class="sec "><p class="kicker">The rules that help you</p><h2>Use the test’s own rules</h2><ul class="points"><li v-click><b>No penalty for wrong answers:</b> never leave a question blank</li><li v-click><b>Mark and Review:</b> you can flag a question and return to it within the same section</li><li v-click><b>Every question counts the same</b> within a section: a hard one is worth no more than an easy one</li><li v-click><b>The first section is medium difficulty;</b> how you do on it sets the difficulty of the second</li></ul></div>

<!--
Because each question is worth the same, a two-minute fight with one question can cost you two easier ones later.
-->

---
layout: none
---

<div class="sec "><p class="kicker">The two-pass approach</p><h2>Go through the section twice</h2><ul class="points"><li v-click><b>Pass 1:</b> answer everything you can do with confidence</li><li v-click>If a question looks long or you’re stuck, put in your best guess, mark it and move on</li><li v-click><b>Pass 2:</b> use the time left on the marked questions</li><li v-click>Before time runs out, every question has an answer</li></ul></div>

---
layout: none
---

<div class="sec "><p class="kicker">The bail-out rule</p><h2>When to let a question go</h2><div class="formula-m"><M t="\text{Stuck at } {\sim}2\text{ minutes} \Rightarrow \text{guess, mark, move}" s="72" c="#1B2433"/></div><p class="explain">Eliminate what you can first: a guess among three choices beats a guess among five.</p><p class="explain">Being stuck is the signal, not the clock: if you have a plan and are making progress, finish it.</p></div>
