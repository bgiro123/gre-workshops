<div class="sec "><p class="kicker">The rules that make pacing work</p><h2>What the test lets you do</h2><ul class="points"><li v-click><b>Any order:</b> within a section, you can move freely between questions</li><li v-click><b>Mark and Review:</b> flag a question and return to it before the section ends</li><li v-click><b>Every question counts the same:</b> a hard one is worth no more than an easy one</li><li v-click><b>No penalty for wrong answers:</b> never leave a question blank</li></ul></div>

<div class="sec "><p class="kicker">Be overly cautious</p><h2>When in doubt, skip it</h2><ul class="points"><li v-click>An easy question is still easy when you come back to it</li><li v-click>The reverse isn’t true: start a hard question by accident and it can eat 4 minutes</li><li v-click>So a quick skip costs little; a wrong start costs a lot</li></ul></div>

---
layout: none
---

<div class="sec prob"><div class="split"><div class="problem"><p class="kicker">Think in general topics</p><h2>Know your strong and weak topics before test day</h2><ul class="points"><li>Strong topic: answer those questions on sight</li><li>Weak topic: flag and skip on the first pass</li><li>Unknown topic: guess and go</li></ul></div><div class="poll"><p class="plabel">Your turn</p><p class="pq">Type one strong topic and one weak topic in the chat.</p><p class="ptip">For example: strong in algebra, weak in probability.</p></div></div></div>

---
layout: none
---

<div class="sec "><p class="kicker">Flag on sight</p><h2>Two question types to save for the second pass</h2><ul class="points"><li v-click><b>Select all that apply:</b> every choice must be checked, so they take longer than they look</li><li v-click><b>Numeric entry:</b> no choices to test or eliminate, so a mistake costs more</li><li v-click>Exception: if one is clearly easy for you, answer it now</li></ul></div>
