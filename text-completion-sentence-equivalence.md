<div class="sec "><p class="kicker">At a glance</p><h2>Two question types, one core skill</h2><div class="cards2"><div class="card" v-click><p class="num">1</p><h3>Text Completion</h3><p class="txt2">One to five sentences with one, two or three blanks. Single blanks have five choices; multi-blank questions have three per blank. Every blank must be right to get credit.</p></div><div class="card" v-click><p class="num">2</p><h3>Sentence Equivalence</h3><p class="txt2">One sentence, one blank, six choices. Both must be right.</p></div></div><p class="foot" v-click>Same skill underneath: work out what the sentence needs before you look at the choices.</p></div>

<div class="sec "><p class="kicker">The pair rule</p><h2>Two right answers that produce the same meaning</h2><ul class="points"><li v-click>Predict the blank first, exactly as in Text Completion</li><li v-click>Find choices that match your prediction, and that match <b>each other</b></li><li v-click>Beware the trap pair: two near-synonyms that don’t fit the sentence</li><li v-click>If only one choice fits, look again: the right answers always come in a pair</li></ul></div>
