<div class="sec "><p class="kicker">The playbook</p><h2>Four moves turn algebra into arithmetic</h2><div class="cards"><div class="card"><p class="num">1</p><h3>Spot it</h3><p class="txt">Variables in the answer choices, or unknowns in a QC, are your signal.</p></div><div class="card"><p class="num">2</p><h3>Read the rules</h3><p class="txt">Note every limit: positive? integer? even? which is bigger?</p></div><div class="card"><p class="num">3</p><h3>Pick friendly values</h3><p class="txt">Small, different from each other, easy to compute. Use 100 for percents.</p></div><div class="card"><p class="num">4</p><h3>Test every choice</h3><p class="txt">More than one survivor? New numbers, and retest only the survivors.</p></div></div><p class="foot">Hard questions are built so a careless number makes two choices look right. Step 4 is where you win.</p></div>
